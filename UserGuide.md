# User Guide
This is a guide on how to use Mogumogu Meal Tracker, a daily meal logging app with a virtual pet system.

## How to Access the App
Open a web browser and enter:
```
http://<server-ip>/index.php
```
---
## Home Page

When you open the app you will see:

- **Pet Status** — your virtual pet and its current health condition
- **Log Meal** — where you can log your meals 
- **Streak** — how many consecutive days you have logged meals
- **Meal History** — access your past logged meals

---

## Logging a Meal

1. Click **Log Meal** in the navigation
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Meal photo** — upload or take a photo of your meal (required)
    - **Date and time** — when you ate your meal (defaults to now)
   - **Meal type** — what type of meal you ate (breakfast,lunch,etc)
   - **Notes** — optional description of your meal

3. Click **Save Meal**

---

## Your Virtual Pet

| Condition | Cause |
|-----------|-------|
| Well Fed   | Meals logged consistently |
| Getting Hungry    | Missed 1 day |
| Very Hungry     | Missed 2 days |
| Critical  | Missed 3 or more days |

> **Note:** For every meal logged, pet health increases by +10%. For every day not logged, pet health decreases by -25%
---

## Streak System

- Logging a meal today adds 1 to your streak
- Missing a day resets your streak back to 0
 - **Longest Streak** — the most number of consecutive days logged
- **Total Days Logged** — the total amount of days logged

---

## Meal History

There are two ways to view your meal history:

### Calendar View

Displays a monthly calendar. Days where you logged a meal show the meal name
directly on the date. Use the **◄** and **►** arrows to navigate between months.

### Gallery View

Displays all logged meals as cards. Each card shows:

- Photo of the meal
- Meal name
- Date logged
- **Edit** button — update the meal name or photo
- **Delete** button — permanently remove the meal entry

> Switch between views using the **Calendar** and **Gallery** buttons at the top
of the page.
---

## Tips
- Log your meal as soon as you eat
- A photo is required — a quick snapshot is enough
- Check your pet daily to stay motivated!
